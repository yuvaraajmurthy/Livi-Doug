Challenge 1

Student-facing app-based digital solution combining student wellness
inventories with personalized, geo-located resources

■ Challenge Summary

Rutgers already runs a Wellness Inventory that gives students a snapshot
of their wellness across eight dimensions, but today that snapshot is
just a number with nothing behind it. This challenge asks teams to build
the missing layer: a conversational, peer-like AI experience that turns
a student's Wellness Inventory results into specific, encouraging next
steps, and pairs each recommendation with a real, nearby place to act on
it, including expanded attention to Newark and Camden, which ScarletWell
has identified as priority areas for growth.

■ Dataset

De-identified Rutgers Wellness Inventory (short form, 18 items, ~5
minutes) results across the eight wellness dimensions (emotional,
environmental, financial, intellectual, occupational, physical, social,
spiritual); related de-identified ScarletWell survey and demographic
data; existing Rutgers wellness resource listings, including the campus
Wellness Calendar and student activity system (The Arc, formerly
GetInvolved); publicly available campus and community location data for
New Brunswick, Newark, and Camden.

■ Challenge Details

Design an AI tool that can:

• Deliver the Wellness Inventory short form as a quick, low-burden
check-in and return personalized, dimension-level feedback in plain,
encouraging language, including both a total score and individual
dimension scores.
• Use a conversational, friend-like tone (not clinical or diagnostic) to
present results and suggest one or two concrete next steps for any
lower-scoring dimension.
• Match a student's specific areas of need to relevant, current campus
resources (counseling, financial aid/wellness, academic support,
recreation, peer groups) rather than a generic resource list.
• Surface nearby wellness supports through geolocation, on campus and in
the surrounding community, with particular attention to Camden and
Newark, so students can find realistic, close-by options.
• (Stretch) Support optional repeat check-ins to show a student's
wellness trends over time, and/or a lightly moderated, anonymous space
for students to share a wellness win with peers.

■ Suggested Approaches

• A retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) approach for the conversational
check-in and feedback layer, grounded in Rutgers-specific resource
content.
• A rules-based or LLM-driven recommendation engine that maps
low-scoring dimensions to specific, current resources and events.
• A mapping or geolocation API layered on top of a campus and community
resource directory, filterable by dimension, cost, and location.
• A simple, mobile-first interface that keeps the check-in and feedback
loop fast and conversational rather than form-like.

■ Deliverables

A working prototype (mobile or web app) that demonstrates: a student
completing the Wellness Inventory check-in; personalized,
dimension-level feedback delivered in a conversational tone; at least
one AI-generated resource recommendation tied to a geolocated, real
Rutgers or community resource; and a short team walkthrough explaining
the approach, data handling/privacy considerations, and how the
prototype could scale across New Brunswick, Newark, and Camden.
